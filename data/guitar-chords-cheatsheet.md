Here you find a list of **guitar open chords**. You can play all of them in the first three frets with no barres.  
With them you play almost any song, both strumming and fingerstyle!

## General tips

All the diagrams refer to the **start of the fretboard**, close to the headstock.

An orange dot means **to place your finger there**, a white dot means **to play the open string**, and the **X symbol** on the string means **not to play it**. Try to avoid strumming it at all, or mute it gently with any free left hand finger if possible.

**Fretting hand fingers** are counted from the index, so:  
**index = 1**, **middle = 2**, **ring = 3**, **pinky = 4**.

Find the most convenient finger position for your hand. Keep the fingertips **as perpendicular as possible** to the fretboard!  
And the left thumb pointing to the sky, not towards the headstock, I see you.

Stop if it hurts. Your hand will adapt with time, do no force any position or motion.

And most important, have fun!
