title: Guitar Chords Cheatsheet
---

All the diagrams refer to the **start of the fretboard**, close to the headstock.

**Fretting hand fingers** are counted from the index, i.e. **index = 1**, **middle = 2**, **ring = 3**, **pinky = 4**.

An **X symbol** on the string means **not to play it**. Try to avoid strumming it, or mute it gently with any free left hand finger if possible.

Find the most convenient finger position for your hand. Keep the fingertips **as perpendicular as possible** to the fretboard!

Stop if it hurts. Your hand will adapt with time, do no force any position or motion.
